🔬 THE 6 EVALUATED BIOMETRIC CANONS

How the Scoring Model Works

1. Bilateral Midline Symmetry (Sagittal Plane)

Calculates lateral variance across left and right landmark pairs relative to the vertical sagittal axis. Evolutionary biology recognizes bilateral symmetry as a primary marker of developmental stability.

2. Golden Ratio Φ (1.618) & Facial Thirds

Compares trichion-to-glabella (upper third), glabella-to-subnasale (midface), and subnasale-to-menton (lower third) against ideal 1:1:1 neoclassical proportions.

3. Jawline Contour & Mandibular Gonial Angle

Measures the intersection angle between the mandibular ramus and mandibular body, evaluating definition and jawline structure.

4. Zygomatic Cheekbone Prominence

Analyzes bizygomatic width relative to bigonial jaw width and midface height, measuring angularity and projection.

5. Canthal Tilt & Ocular Palpebral Inclination

Evaluates the vector from medial canthus to lateral canthus relative to the pupillary horizontal line, identifying positive, neutral, or negative slant.

6. Biological Age & Tissue Vitality

Multi-factor assessment of periorbital volume, nasolabial depth, and structural tone providing an estimated biological vitality age.
